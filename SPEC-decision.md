# Spec: decision (P4)

Shared rules: [SPEC-P3.md](SPEC-P3.md). PRD 0.6 P4: funding or no-funding decision.

## Objective
The owner makes the continue / seek funding / stop call from one page of facts.

## Scope
- `docs/p4-decision.md`, one page:
  - the gate results for S1–S4, P1, P2 and P3, with numbers and links;
  - what production would take: open items (Galaxy A23, model hosting, in-app download), estimated costs [VERIFY each], and risks;
  - three options: continue solo, seek funding, stop. Each option lists what it needs and the evidence that would change the call.
- **Funding criteria:** asked of the owner when the memo is drafted (open question 5 in SPEC-P3.md).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every fact in the memo links to a source file.
2. The owner's decision is recorded in Decisions as a dated line.

## Out of scope
A pitch deck and investor outreach. Those come after a "seek funding" decision, under a new decision entry.
